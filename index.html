About Cornelle's Seafoods

We are committed to providing quality products and reliable service to our valued customers.

Through our dedication to quality, consistency, and customer satisfaction, we continue to build trusted partnerships with the businesses and communities we serve.

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

To serve the country as a significant contributor to the national food industry by being a respectable food processor.

Our Vision

  • To be adaptable and flexible in meeting customers' demand for quality food products.
  • Be the source of inspiration for commitment, industriousness, and excellence among the workforce. To alleviate the economic status of farming and fishing communities.
  • Always strive for excellence and economic gain without sacrificing our mission to be of service to the country.
